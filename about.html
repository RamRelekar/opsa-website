About OPSA

Online Platform Services Available

OPSA is an online platform that connects customers with independent service professionals. It starts with two things people in Mumbai need often and find hard to arrange: reliable hospitality staff for events and hotels, and fixed-price home services.

OPSA grew out of years of hands-on experience supplying waiters, bartenders and butlers to caterers and events. We saw how hard it is for customers to find dependable staff at short notice, and how hard it is for good workers to find steady, fairly paid work. OPSA is built to fix both sides.

What we stand for

  • Verified professionals. Service providers complete identity verification before they can take bookings.
  • Clear prices. You see the price before you book.
  • Safe payments. Payments are processed by Razorpay and released to the professional after the job is completed.
  • Fair work. Professionals are paid on time for every completed booking.

Who runs OPSA

OPSA is operated by Shankar Relekar, based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. For any question, write to support@opsa.in.
