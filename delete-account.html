Delete Your Account

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can ask OPSA to delete your account and personal data at any time. This page explains how, and what we keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@opsa.in with the subject “Delete my OPSA account”.
  2. Send it from the email address or phone number registered with your account, so we can match it. Do not share OTPs or passwords.
  3. We confirm by email once your account is deleted. We process requests within 30 days.

Once the in-app option is live, you can also delete your account from the app under Profile.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email, profile photo and address
  • Your phone number, which is replaced so it no longer identifies you
  • Your saved favourites, device push tokens and location history
  • Your login sessions, which end immediately

What we keep, and why

  • Payment and payout records: needed for tax, accounting and dispute records, for as long as the law requires.
  • KYC and verification records (masked identity numbers only): kept for legal compliance, as set out in our Privacy Policy.

Before you delete

  • Finish or cancel any booking or job in progress. We cannot delete an account with active work or held payments.
  • Withdraw any balance you are owed before deletion, or we will pay it to the bank or UPI details on file before closing the account.
