Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OPSA (“OPSA”, “we”, “us”), operated by Shankar Relekar, collects, uses and protects personal data when you use the OPSA website at opsa.in and the OPSA mobile apps (together, the “Platform”). We process personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law.

1. Data we collect

Customers

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Service address and location you share for a booking
  • Booking history, ratings and messages with support

Service professionals

  • Name, phone, email, photo, address
  • Identity and KYC details needed for verification (such as Aadhaar or PAN), checked through a licensed verification provider
  • Bank or UPI details for payouts
  • Location during an active job, work history and ratings

Payment data

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN. We receive only a payment reference, status and amount.

Device data

Device type, app version, IP address and crash logs, used to keep the Platform secure and working.

2. How we use your data

  • To create your account and confirm bookings
  • To match customers with nearby professionals
  • To verify the identity of professionals and keep the Platform safe
  • To process payments, refunds and payouts
  • To send booking updates, OTPs and support replies
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal and tax requirements

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share data with

  • The other party to a booking: a professional sees the customer’s name, service address and booking details; the customer sees the professional’s name, photo and rating.
  • Service providers that help us run the Platform, such as Razorpay (payments and payouts), identity verification providers, cloud hosting, SMS and email providers. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect someone’s safety.

4. How long we keep data

We keep account data while your account is active. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 8 years). KYC records are kept as required by law. Other data is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to support@opsa.in. Some data may need to be kept where the law requires it.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and regular reviews to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please keep your OTPs and passwords private.

7. Children

The Platform is meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact and grievances

Grievance officer: Shankar Relekar, support@opsa.in, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
