Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for bookings made on OPSA (opsa.in and the OPSA apps).

1. Cancellation by the customer

Home services, cancelled 2 hours or more before the start timeFull refund
Home services, cancelled less than 2 hours before the start timeRefund minus a cancellation fee of up to ₹100
Staff hire, cancelled 24 hours or more before the shiftFull refund
Staff hire, cancelled less than 24 hours before the shiftRefund minus up to 25% of the booking value
Cancelled after the Professional has arrived or the shift has startedNo refund for the time already booked

2. Cancellation by OPSA or the Professional

If a Professional cancels, does not arrive, or we cannot find a Professional for your booking, you get a full refund, or we will offer a replacement if you prefer.

3. Problems with the service

If the work was not done as booked, write to support@opsa.in within 48 hours of the service with your booking ID and photos if possible. After review we will arrange a free redo or a partial or full refund, depending on what happened.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) through Razorpay.
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of approval. It usually reaches your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • We do not refund in cash.

5. Service delivery

OPSA sells services, not physical goods, so there is no shipping. Services are delivered at the address and time you choose when booking, within the service areas shown in the app.

6. Contact

For any cancellation or refund question: support@opsa.in · +91 XXXXX XXXXX
